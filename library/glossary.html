The FCL handbook sets no fixed day count for the second deadline: it is the due date in DCSA’s investigation email. Cleared contractors follow the DAAG via NISP eMASS (DCSA NCSO). A GCA or a currently cleared contractor may sponsor (32 CFR 117.9(a)(10)). The pre-2025 SF 328 threshold and “foreign proceeds” wording are unverified and were removed.
