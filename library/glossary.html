GLOSSARY — DCSA (Defense Counterintelligence and Security Agency)

Glossary for DCSA dual-track FCL/PCL fluency.

Term Meaning in this pack Block
DCSA Defense Counterintelligence and Security Agency — DoD agency administering NISP for industry, personnel vetting, industrial security, CI support, and related IS authorization oversight 1
NISP National Industrial Security Program — framework for protecting classified information in industry 1
NISPOM National Industrial Security Program Operating Manual — codified as 32 CFR Part 117 (governing rule for cleared contractors) [S1] 1
NISS National Industrial Security System — DCSA system of record for FCL sponsorship, packages, and industrial security casework 1
FCL Facility Clearance — authorization for a company/entity to access classified information at a stated level; cannot be self-sponsored 1
PCL Personnel Clearance — individual eligibility for classified access after investigation and adjudication 1
GCA Government Contracting Activity — government organization that (with cleared primes) can initiate FCL sponsorship 1
DD-441 DD Form 441 — Security Agreement between the US Government and the contractor under the NISP 1
SF-328 Certificate Pertaining to Foreign Interests — FOCI disclosure form; May 2025 update lowered foreign revenue/proceeds threshold (Q7b) to 15% [S3][S4] 1,5
FOCI Foreign Ownership, Control, or Influence — condition that can block or delay FCL until mitigated (e.g. SCA, SSA, Proxy Board) 1,5
KMP Key Management Personnel — officers/directors and designated security roles (e.g. FSO) who must be cleared or excluded for FCL 1,3
SF-86 Questionnaire for National Security Positions — personal history form submitted via e-QIP / NBIS for PCL 1
NBIS National Background Investigation Services — modern personnel vetting IT suite replacing legacy e-QIP pathways over time 1
Tier 3 Investigation tier typically supporting Secret / Confidential — national agency checks, credit, ~5 years history 1
Tier 5 Investigation tier typically supporting Top Secret — full field investigation, ~10 years history, subject interviews 1
SEAD 4 Security Executive Agent Directive 4 — thirteen adjudicative guidelines under the whole-person concept (not a pass/fail checklist) 1,2
Continuous vetting Ongoing automated/record checks after clearance grant; NISP contractor model updated toward PVQ cadence (e.g. every 5 years) with periodic reinvestigation eliminated for many NISP contractors [S9] 1,5
ISR Industrial Security Representative — DCSA field contact for the cleared facility’s industrial security posture 1
Possessing FCL Facility authorized to store/process classified information on-site (higher physical/IS obligations) 1,5
Non-possessing FCL Facility cleared for access (e.g. at other locations) without storing classified on-site — still needs sponsorship, KMPs, and FSO program 1,5
DAAPM DCSA Assessment and Authorization Process Manual — RMF-aligned process for authorizing classified contractor information systems [S13] 1,5
CDSE Center for Development of Security Excellence — DCSA training organization 1,7
STEPP Security Training, Education, and Professionalization Portal — CDSE learning portal 1,7
FSO Facility Security Officer — contractor security lead for NISP compliance at the facility 1
CMMC (contrast) Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification — uncleared/CUI contractor floor; ≠ FCL and does not authorize Secret/TS — see sibling pack 2,5
