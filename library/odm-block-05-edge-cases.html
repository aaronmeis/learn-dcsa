Block 5: Edge cases

Time: 45 minutes
Goal: Exceptions, traps, failure modes
Pack: DCSA (Defense Counterintelligence and Security Agency) (dcsa)

Focus from mind map

  • Self-sponsorship myths
  • Confusing CMMC / GCC High / FedRAMP with Secret/TS FCL or system authorization
  • SF 328 “no FOCI” treated as permanent — material changes require update
  • International university partnerships / foreign students / foreign grants under SF 328 Q7 scope (15%)
  • Non-possessing vs possessing FCL obligations
  • FOCI mitigation timeline risk (SCA / SSA / Proxy, etc.)
  • Continuous vetting vs old periodic reinvestigation mental model [S9]
  • Classified IS: DAAG/RMF ≠ CMMC [S13][S14]

Sources

  • [S3][S4] SF 328 May 2025 announcement + industry paper
  • [S9] DCSA continuous vetting update (May 2026)
  • [S12] FOCI and Supply Chain Risk Management webinar
  • [S13] DAAPM v2.2 (historical; now the DAAG)
  • [S14] CDSE CS250 — Applying A&A in the NISP
  • Sibling: CMMC cheatsheet (contrast)

Actions

  1. Build a trap table (symptom → cause → fix) for at least these eight:
Trap Symptom Cause Fix
Self-sponsor NISS filing with no GCA/prime Myth that company can initiate Get sponsorship from GCA or currently cleared contractor
Clock theater Fast incomplete package Clock vs quality inverted Complete Day 20 package; queue DCSA due-date KMPs
CMMC = FCL “We’re L2 / on GCC High” Wrong wall Separate CUI floor from NISP FCL/ATO
SF 328 stale Old “no FOCI” after new foreign revenue Threshold/material change ignored Re-file; note 15% Q7b [S4]
University FOCI blind spot Foreign grants/students unexamined Research partnerships out of mind Map revenue + influence to SF 328 questions
Non-possessing complacency “We don’t store classified, so light duties” Under-scoped FSO obligations Still need FCL governance + PCL pipeline
Possessing without DAAG CMMC cert waved at classified IS Equating cyber floors DAAG/RMF A&A for NISP systems
CV lag Waiting for old PR cycle only Outdated reinvestigation model Continuous vetting + PVQ cadence [S9]
  1. Add the top three traps to cheatsheet.
  2. Write one paragraph: how a lab with foreign research revenue near 15% should brief leadership this quarter.

Retrieval check

Question

What edge case would break a naive “submit once and forget” approach to SF 328? What breaks “we have GCC High so we’re cleared”?

%% fold answer after attempt %%

Answer cues

Material change / foreign revenue crossing 15% (or new foreign ownership/control) requires update — SF 328 is not permanent [S3][S4]. GCC High supports certain CUI architectures; it does not grant FCL or authorize Secret/TS — wrong wall / CMMC≠FCL.

Exit criteria

  • ≥8 traps with symptom/cause/fix
  • Top three on cheatsheet
  • FOCI leadership paragraph written
