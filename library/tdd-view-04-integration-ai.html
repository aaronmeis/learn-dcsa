Third-party and external systems

Required section

Every system outside the company FSO/ISSM boundary that sends data in, receives data out, or governs eligibility. Government endpoints are named. Commercial GRC/AI vendors are categories with silence where no product was researched. Identity and access

  • Sponsor, FSO, ISSM, KMP subjects, and adjudicators hold distinct roles; sponsorship credentials are not company self-service admin rights. [S2]
  • SF-86-class and FOCI datasets require need-to-know inside the company before any tool integration.
  • Cloud IAM (including GCC High) authorizes hosting, not NISP eligibility.
  • Silence: exact NISS role matrix beyond FSO/sponsor patterns taught in CDSE courses.

AI elements

AI boundary (affirmative)

AI assists FSO workflows (checklists, training Q&A, document triage on non-PII). AI does not replace sponsorship, adjudication, or FOCI determination. SF-86-class and FOCI PII stay out of consumer AI tools unless a written gov-approved pattern exists. Official DCSA AI tooling inventory: silence.
