Example DCSA journey plan

Mock project plan from sponsorship to continuous vetting: phases, owners, durations, decision points and a gantt-style view.

Example plan, not an official timeline. This is a mock project plan for study. DCSA sets real deadlines in its own emails and in NISS. Where the sources give no number, this page says “varies”. Check current guidance at dcsa.mil/FCL before planning real work.

Phase 1: Sponsorship

Owner: Sponsor (GCA or currently cleared contractor); company provides info · Duration: Varies

  1. Confirm a classified contract need exists (a GCA or cleared prime has the requirement).
  2. Sponsor submits the FCL sponsorship in NISS. The company cannot sponsor itself S15 S2.
  3. DCSA accepts the sponsorship; the FSO receives the welcome email S2.
Decision point. Gate A: Is there a sponsor with a legitimate need? If no, stop. There is no self-sponsor path.

Phase 2: FCL package

Owner: Company: SMO, FSO, legal/corporate · Duration: Day 1 to Day 20 (welcome email = Day 1)

  1. Get NISS access and read the welcome email deadlines S2.
  2. Assemble the package: formation documents, ownership chart, DD Form 441, SF 328 (form approved May 2025), KMP list S2 S3 S4.
  3. Name KMPs by security-affecting authority (SMO, FSO, ITPSO at minimum), not by title S2.
  4. Submit the package in NISS by Day 20 S2.
Decision point. Gate B: Does SF 328 show foreign ownership, control or influence? If yes, add a FOCI mitigation track (duration varies) S24 S4.

Phase 3: KMP clearances (PCL) in DISS / eApp

Owner: DCSA initiates for initial FCL; KMPs complete eApp; FSO handles fingerprints · Duration: Due date set in DCSA's investigation email (varies)

  1. For the initial FCL, DCSA initiates KMP investigations; KMPs complete the SF-86 in eApp S2.
  2. Fingerprints go through SWFT by the same due date S25.
  3. Since Jul 31, 2026, industry initiates and reviews eApp cases in DISS; NBIS Agency stops new cases Sep 30 and turns off Nov 30, 2026 S16.
  4. Adjudication uses the 13 SEAD 4 guidelines S22.
Decision point. Gate C: Are all required KMPs eligible (or excluded by board resolution where allowed)? If not, the FCL waits S2.

Phase 4: System authorization (only if possessing / processing classified)

Owner: FSO + ISSM; DCSA NCSO · Duration: Varies

  1. Decide possessing vs. non-possessing. Non-possessing still needs cleared KMPs and an FSO S7.
  2. If classified systems are needed, follow the DAAG and submit RMF packages through NISP eMASS S13 S14.
  3. CMMC or GCC High do not substitute for an FCL or a classified ATO S18.
Decision point. Gate D: Will the company process classified information on its own systems? If no, skip this phase.

Phase 5: FCL grant

Owner: DCSA · Duration: Varies

  1. DCSA makes the entity eligibility determination once the package, KMP eligibility and any FOCI mitigation are complete S1 S2.
  2. Record status in NISS; keep the DD 441 and KMP list current S1.
Decision point. Gate E: Granted, or returned with deficiencies. A return restarts work on the gap, not the whole journey.

Phase 6: Operate: continuous vetting and reporting

Owner: FSO, ITPSO, all cleared staff · Duration: Ongoing

  1. Cleared people stay enrolled in continuous vetting S9.
  2. Periodic reinvestigations are no longer required for NISP contractors: submit an updated PVQ (SF-86 in eApp) every five years, timed from the PVQ Date in DISS S9.
  3. Report changed conditions and required events under 32 CFR 117.8 and SEAD 3 S20 S21.
  4. Run the insider threat program and annual self-inspection; complete required CDSE FSO training S1 S7 S8.
  5. If cleared staff only work at other sites, NAESOC may be the oversight office S23.
Decision point. Gate F: Any changed condition (ownership, KMP, address)? Report it and update NISS.
