Mind map — DCSA (tech deep dive)

Mode A from user dual-track brief. Architecture lens: conceptual / logical / physical / integration. Nodes tagged [verify] need primary-doc confirmation.

DCSA — Defense Counterintelligence and Security Agency

  • What it is: DoD agency administering the National Industrial Security Program (NISP) for most cleared industry, plus personnel vetting / continuous vetting and related CI mission elements.
  • Central thesis: Two interlocking tracks — facility eligibility (FCL) and personnel eligibility (PCL) — plus IS authorization (DAAG/RMF) and counterintelligence. The tracks feed each other; neither is sufficient alone.
  • Out of scope for this day: Full CMMC control memorization; SAP/SCI special-access deep dive; building a classified enclave from scratch (point to sibling packs).

Block 1 — Context and scope

  • Stakeholders: GCA, currently cleared contractor, uncleared applicant company, FSO/ISSM, KMP, ISR (Industrial Security Representative), adjudicators, CDSE trainers
  • Need: legitimate need for classified access; US organization; integrity record (32 CFR 117.9 eligibility themes)
  • Cannot self-sponsor into NISP — sponsorship via NISS by GCA or currently cleared contractor
  • Adjacent regimes: CMMC (CUI floor), FedRAMP/GCC High (cloud support ≠ FCL), JSIG/ICD for SCI spaces

Block 2 — Conceptual view (capabilities & actors)

  • Capabilities: sponsor → package → FOCI assess → investigate KMPs → grant/maintain FCL; initiate PCL → investigate → adjudicate (SEAD 4) → continuous vetting; authorize contractor IS; CI/insider-threat reporting culture
  • Actors & trust boundaries: USG sponsor | contractor legal entity | foreign interest (FOCI) | cleared individual | uncleared staff | academic partner
  • Four domains: 1. Industrial security / NISP 2. Personnel vetting 3. IS authorization (DAAG + RMF) 4. Counterintelligence
  • Layer cake under each: governing regs → DCSA handbooks → CDSE courses → hands-on practice

Block 3 — Logical view (process & data)

  • FCL lifecycle: Sponsorship accepted (welcome email Day 1) → Day 20 package → DCSA completeness cycles (limited resubmit attempts) → DCSA due-date KMP eApp/fingerprints → FOCI determination / mitigation → FCL issued → recurring ISR review + self-inspection
  • Data entities: NISS facility record, DD 441 agreement, SF 328 certificate, ownership chart, KMP roster, PCL case (SF-86), adjudication record, FOCI mitigation instrument, system authorization package
  • PCL tiers: Tier 3 (Secret/Confidential) vs Tier 5 (Top Secret) — scope of investigation and history windows
  • SEAD 4: thirteen adjudicative guidelines; whole-person concept
  • Clocks: Day 20 and DCSA due-date hard gates; FOCI mitigation adds months

Block 4 — Physical view (systems & artifacts)

  • Systems of record: NISS (facility), eApp (DISS) (investigations), DISS (personnel eligibility records — confirm current stack) [verify], STEPP (training)
  • Forms & artifacts: DD 441, SF 328 (May 2025 revision), SF-86, fingerprints, formation docs, board minutes, stock ledgers
  • SF 328 2025 physical change: Q7(b) reporting threshold 15%; expanded gifts/tuition/endowments; Statement of Full Disclosure of Foreign Affiliations for foreign employment of management; NISS deployed 12 May 2025
  • On-site: possessing vs non-possessing FCL; storage containers; classified processing rooms; visitor control
  • IS path: DAAG overlays RMF for NISP contractor systems — ATO artifacts, ISSM/ISSO roles

Block 5 — Integration and AI elements

  • External parties: GCA program offices, primes/subs, DCSA field (ISR), adjudication community, CDSE, fingerprint providers, cloud/hosting vendors (when CUI or classified processing involved)
  • Integrations: contract DD Form 254 → access need; HR ↔ FSO adverse-info handoff; legal/ownership ↔ FOCI; ISSM ↔ FSO for system boundaries
  • AI elements (honest boundary): AI may assist FSO workflows (triage docs, training Q&A, package checklists) but does not replace sponsorship, adjudication, or FOCI determination. Mark gov-cloud / data-handling limits for any AI tooling touching SF-86-class PII or FOCI data. Silence: official DCSA AI tooling inventory.
