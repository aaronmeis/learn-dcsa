DCSA (Defense Counterintelligence and Security Agency) — One-Day Mastery Hub

Slug: dcsa · NBLM alias: odm-dcsa

TL;DR

DCSA sits at the center of two interlocking tracks: Facility Clearance (FCL) under NISPOM 32 CFR Part 117 via NISS sponsorship (GCA or cleared prime — no self-sponsor), and Personnel Clearance (PCL) via SF-86 / NBIS with Tier 3 (Secret) vs Tier 5 (TS) and SEAD 4 thirteen whole-person guidelines. Hard clocks after welcome email: Day 20 package · Day 45 KMP investigations + fingerprints. SF 328 (May 2025) lowered foreign revenue disclosure to 15%. Training hub: CDSE / STEPP. Four domains: industrial security/NISP · personnel vetting · DAAPM/RMF IS auth · counterintelligence. Falsifier: still thinking a company can self-sponsor, or that GCC High / CMMC = FCL.

Siblings: Tech deep dive (architecture day) · CMMC lab-floor pack (CMMC floor stays separate from FCL).
