ODM mission

Mission for one-day mastery of DCSA dual-track FCL/PCL fluency.

Mission — DCSA (Defense Counterintelligence and Security Agency)

Why

One focused day to reach dual-track working fluency: Facility Clearance (FCL) under NISPOM 32 CFR Part 117 via NISS sponsorship, and Personnel Clearance (PCL) via SF-86 / NBIS with Tier 3 vs Tier 5 and SEAD 4 adjudication — treated as one interlocking operating model (industrial security, personnel vetting, DAAPM/RMF IS authorization, counterintelligence), not “how to get a badge.”

Sibling contrast: CMMC lab-floor pack — CMMC ≠ FCL.

Success looks like

  • Can state the dual-track lock without notes: no useful PCL pipeline without an eligible facility sponsor; no usable FCL without cleared KMPs / need-to-know population
  • Can recite the sponsorship rule: a company cannot self-sponsor; a GCA or cleared prime initiates sponsorship in NISS
  • Can name Day 20 (governance/forms package) and Day 45 (KMP investigation requests + fingerprints) clocks from welcome-email Day 1, and list package contents (formation docs, ownership chart, DD-441, SF-328, KMP list)
  • Can contrast Tier 3 (Secret/Confidential) vs Tier 5 (Top Secret) investigation depth and name SEAD 4 as thirteen whole-person guidelines (not a checklist)
  • Can map the four domains to CDSE/STEPP courses (IS140, IS142, FOCI, self-inspection, DAAPM/CS250)
  • Can state SF 328 May 2025 change: foreign revenue / proceeds disclosure threshold lowered to 15% (from 30%)
  • Can teach the dual-track swimlane in ~10 minutes and ship Block 7 artifacts (swimlane + FOCI/SF 328 one-pager + CDSE course map)

Out of scope

  • Full CMMC control dump or treating CMMC / GCC High / FedRAMP as FCL or Secret/TS ATO
  • SAP / SCI / compartmented program deep dive (name the contrast only)
  • Multi-week FSO academy or memorizing every NISPOM subsection
  • Inventing or requesting API keys / live NISS submissions

Constraints

  • Time box: ~350 focused minutes (standard seven-block day)
  • Ground claims in RESOURCES / source-ledger ([S1]–[S14])
  • Prefer process swimlanes and package drills over regulation grind
  • Falsifier: ending the day still believing a company can self-sponsor an FCL, or that GCC High / CMMC / FedRAMP equals FCL (or authorizes Secret/TS)
