TDD day blocks

Block 6: Hands-on lab

Block 6 — hands-on tabletop lab: Day-20 package, SF 328 Q7 near 15%, scenario sort, SEAD 4 mini, CDSE map.

Time: 60 minutes · Goal: Tabletop the dual-track artifacts — no live classified data, no real PII

Work mode: Document / whiteboard lab (not a production NISS tenant). Save outputs under assets/ if you capture photos of the board.

Focus from mind map

  • Reconstruct a Day-20 package checklist for a fictional LLC seeking Secret FCL via prime sponsorship
  • Map SF 328 Q7 for a university lab with foreign grants near the 15% line
  • Sort three scenarios: CMMC-only feeder vs NISP possessing facility vs visiting researcher under sponsor FCL
  • Draft a SEAD 4 whole-person mini-adjudication (open-source vignette only)
  • Build a CDSE course map: IS011, IS140, IS142, IS065, IS130 → domains

Sources

  • [S2] FCL handbook · [S4] SF 328 industry paper · [S5]–[S8] CDSE · [S6] SEAD 4 via IS142 · 02 - Logical View · 03 - Physical View

Actions (tied to views)

  • Lab A — Day-20 checklist: Using logical entities and forms, list every artifact for a fictional US LLC, Secret, non-possessing, sponsored by a currently cleared contractor. Include DD 441, SF 328, ownership, KMP, formation docs.
  • Lab B — FOCI near 15%: For a fictional university lab with foreign grants/gifts, compute whether Q7(b) reporting approach the 15% line; note gifts/tuition/endowments expanded in 2025. [S4] Do not use real institutional financials.
  • Lab C — Scenario sort: Label each card: (1) CMMC-only CUI feeder system, (2) NISP possessing facility, (3) visiting researcher under sponsor FCL. State which clocks and systems apply.
  • Lab D — SEAD 4 tabletop: One open-source vignette; map to two or three of the thirteen guidelines; conclude whole-person — not a real case.
  • Lab E — CDSE map: Place IS140, IS142, IS020.CU, CS250 (and optional IS011/IS065/IS130) onto the four domains.

Retrieval check

Question

What did the lab show that the documentation alone did not?

Answer cues

Expect: (1) KMP list is harder than titles — security-affecting authority drives who enters DCSA due-date; (2) university foreign-grant math under 15% forces finance + FSO collaboration; (3) CMMC-only scenario has no NISS sponsorship clock; (4) SEAD 4 resists single-issue thinking. Record your actual board photos or checklist file path here after the lab.

Exit criteria

  • Labs A–E completed with fictional data only; at least one artifact saved or photographed under assets/.
