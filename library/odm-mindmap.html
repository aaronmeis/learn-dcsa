Mind map — DCSA (one-day mastery)

Mode A from user dual-track brief. Agency + interlocking FCL/PCL process. Nodes tagged [verify] need primary-doc confirmation in the research pass.

DCSA (center)

  • Thesis: DCSA sits at the center of two parallel tracks that feed each other — facility clearance (FCL) and personnel clearance (PCL). Neither works without the other.
  • Audience framing: Learn the agency system as an operating model (NISP admin, vetting, IS authorization, CI), not only as “how to get a badge.”
  • Career path angle: CDSE / STEPP → FSO curriculum → shadowing in a cleared environment.

Track 1 — Facility (lab / company cleared)

  • Governing doc: NISPOM = 32 CFR Part 117
  • Sponsorship rule people miss: A company cannot sponsor itself. A Government Contracting Activity (GCA) or currently cleared contractor initiates sponsorship in NISS (National Industrial Security System).
  • Hard clock after acceptance (welcome email = Day 1):
  • Day 20: governance docs + forms package in NISS
  • DCSA due-date: Key Management Personnel (KMP) investigation requests + fingerprints
  • Package contents:
  • Formation / legal docs
  • Ownership chart
  • DD Form 441 — Security Agreement
  • SF 328 — Certificate Pertaining to Foreign Interests (FOCI disclosure)
  • KMP list
  • Failure mode: DCSA returns a large share of first packages as incomplete — documentation quality is the main choke point [verify exact fraction]
  • FOCI gate: Foreign Ownership, Control, or Influence; mitigation (SCA / SSA / Proxy, etc.) can add 6–12 months [verify typical ranges]
  • SF 328 May 2025 revision: Q7(b) reporting threshold lowered to 15%; NISS live ~12 May 2025 — material for labs with international research partnerships

Track 2 — Individual (person cleared)

  • Start: SF-86 via eApp / NBIS
  • Tier 3 → Secret / Confidential: national agency check, credit, ~5 years history
  • Tier 5 → Top Secret: full field investigation, subject interviews
  • Adjudication: SEAD 4 — thirteen guidelines (whole-person concept, not a checklist): 1. Allegiance to the United States 2. Foreign influence 3. Foreign preference 4. Sexual behavior 5. Personal conduct 6. Financial considerations 7. Alcohol consumption 8. Drug involvement and substance misuse 9. Psychological conditions 10. Criminal conduct 11. Handling protected information 12. Outside activities 13. Use of information technology systems
  • After grant: continuous vetting; periodic security questionnaire cadence (~every 5 years historically; confirm current CV model) [verify]

Four learning domains (branch from DCSA)

  1. Industrial security / NISP — NISPOM, NISS, FCL, DD 441, FOCI, ISR relationship
  2. Personnel vetting — SF-86, tiers, SEAD 4, DISS/NBIS, continuous vetting
  3. Information systems authorization — DAAG + RMF for classified contractor systems
  4. Counterintelligence — threat reporting, insider threat, foreign contact/travel culture

Under each domain (layer cake)

  • Governing regs / EOs / SEADs
  • DCSA handbooks (e.g. FCL Orientation Handbook)
  • CDSE courses (map IS140 / IS142 / FOCI / self-inspection to FCL & PCL)
  • Hands-on practice — shadow an FSO or work inside a cleared environment

Training hub (career path)

  • CDSE — Center for Development of Security Excellence
  • Portal: STEPP
  • FSO path: curricula such as non-possessing FSO orientation (~26+ hours of eLearning in IS020.CU) and broader FSO toolkit across areas: facility clearance, personnel security, information security, insider threat, cybersecurity, classification management, self-inspection [verify “40 hours / seven areas” packaging]
  • Named courses: Facility Clearances in the NISP, Personnel Clearances in the NISP (IS142.16), Understanding FOCI, NISP Self-Inspection, Getting Started Seminar for new FSOs

Mental models for the day

  • Dual-track lock: no useful PCL pipeline without an eligible facility sponsor; no usable FCL without cleared KMPs / need-to-know population
  • Clock vs quality: Day 20 and DCSA due-date clocks punish incomplete packages harder than slow perfect packages
  • FOCI as schedule risk: disclosure threshold changes (15%) expand who must explain foreign revenue / partnerships
  • Whole person vs checklist: SEAD 4 adjudication is cumulative and contextual
  • CMMC ≠ FCL: uncleared CUI floor is a different wall from NISP classified ATO (cross-link existing CMMC lab-floor pack)

Edge cases / falsifiers

  • Self-sponsorship myths
  • Confusing CMMC / GCC High / FedRAMP with Secret/TS FCL or system authorization
  • Treating SF 328 “no FOCI” as permanent — material changes require update
  • International university partnerships / foreign students / foreign grants under new SF 328 Q7 scope
  • Non-possessing vs possessing FCL obligations

Application / teach-back

  • Draw the dual-track swimlane (GCA/prime → NISS → Day 20 and DCSA due-date → KMP PCLs → FCL grant)
  • One-page FOCI / SF 328 change brief for a lab with foreign research revenue near 15%
  • CDSE course map tied to the four domains
