Block 4: Teach it

Time: 30 minutes
Goal: Explain aloud or write a teach-back
Pack: DCSA (Defense Counterintelligence and Security Agency) (dcsa)

Focus from mind map

  • Dual-track thesis in plain language for a sharp beginner (lab director / founder / new FSO)
  • Must include: no self-sponsor · Day 20/45 · Tier 3 vs 5 · SEAD 4 whole-person · SF 328 15% · CMMC ≠ FCL
  • Career path pointer: CDSE/STEPP → FSO curriculum → shadowing

Sources

  • Your Block 1–3 notes + cheatsheet
  • [S10] Bennett FSO talk (structure cues)
  • [S5][S6] CDSE IS140 / IS142 titles for the course map close

Actions

  1. Teach aloud 8–10 minutes (or write a teach-back of equal density) covering: - What DCSA runs (four domains in one sentence each) - FCL path: GCA/prime → NISS → Day 20 package → Day 45 KMPs → grant - PCL path: SF-86/NBIS → Tier 3 vs Tier 5 → SEAD 4 adjudication → continuous vetting - FOCI / SF 328 15% as schedule risk - One falsifier kill: self-sponsor myth or GCC High = FCL
  2. Close with a 30-second CDSE pointer (IS140, IS142, IS020.CU / FSO Toolkit).
  3. Record gaps you stalled on; fix with a short source check — not a full re-read.

Suggested outline (optional)

  1. Hook — “Clearance is two tracks that lock.”
  2. Facility — sponsorship + clocks + package five-pack
  3. People — tiers + SEAD 4
  4. Risk — FOCI / 15%
  5. Not that wall — CMMC/GCC High
  6. Next learning — STEPP

Retrieval check

Question

Explain dual-track DCSA to a sharp beginner in under two minutes. Where did you stall?

%% fold answer after attempt %%

Answer cues

Pass if sponsorship rule, Day 20/45, and at least one of SEAD 4 / 15% / CMMC≠FCL appear without notes. Common stalls: KMP definition, possessing vs non-possessing, DAAPM vs CMMC.

Exit criteria

  • Teach-back done (audio or written)
  • Gaps listed and spot-fixed
