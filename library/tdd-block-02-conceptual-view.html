Block 2: Conceptual view

Block 2 — conceptual view: four domains, actors, capabilities, OV-1 dual-track (no product names).

Time: 60 minutes · Goal: Capabilities, actors, and value, with no products named

Work in: Conceptual View

Focus from mind map

  • Four domains: industrial security / NISP; personnel vetting; IS authorization (DAAPM + RMF); counterintelligence
  • Capabilities: sponsor → package → FOCI → KMP investigate → grant FCL; PCL → investigate → SEAD 4 → CV; authorize IS; CI culture
  • Actors & trust: USG sponsor | contractor | foreign interest | cleared individual | uncleared staff | academic partner
  • Altitude rule: no product names in this view

Sources

  • [S1] 32 CFR 117 · [S2] FCL handbook · [S5][S6] IS140/IS142 · [S12] FOCI webinar · [S13][S14] DAAPM / CS250

Actions (tied to view)

  • Fill Actors and Capabilities (C1–C10) tables in 01 - Conceptual View — confirm they already match the mind map; correct any drift.
  • Redraw the OV-1 dual-track mermaid from memory on paper, then compare to the view (facility track ↔ personnel track, FOCI branch, IS auth, CI).
  • Recite the four domains and the layer cake under each (rule → handbook → CDSE → practice).
  • Test: remove every product/portal name from your notes; if a sentence breaks, move it to the physical view.
  • Mark open questions that remain [verify] (Day-20 resubmit count; CV/PVQ cadence).

Retrieval check

Question

Which capability would a sponsor miss first if it vanished, and why?

Answer cues

C1 Sponsorship intake — without a GCA or cleared-prime sponsorship path, no legitimate FCL case starts; companies cannot self-sponsor into the NISP. [S2][S10] Close second: C3 FOCI assessment, because foreign influence blocks eligibility even when the package is otherwise complete.

Exit criteria

  • Conceptual view complete with no product names; dual-track OV-1 drawable from memory.
