Quiz bank — DCSA (FCL / PCL)

Answer closed-book. Check cues only after attempting. Site #/quiz mirrors this bank.

Q1

Can a company self-sponsor an FCL in NISS? Who initiates sponsorship?

Cue

No. A Government Contracting Activity (GCA) or a cleared prime initiates sponsorship in NISS [S2][S10].

Q2

Welcome email is Day 1. What is due by Day 20? By Day 45?

Cue

Day 20: Five-Pack in NISS (formation docs, ownership chart, DD-441, SF-328, KMP list). Day 45: KMP investigation requests + fingerprints [S2].

Q3

Name the five core items in a typical FCL package (Five-Pack).

Cue

Formation/legal docs · ownership chart · DD Form 441 · SF 328 · KMP list.

Q4

Name three minimum KMP roles cleared to the FCL level.

Cue

SMO (Senior Management Official), FSO, ITPSO (Insider Threat Program Senior Official).

Q5

What investigation tiers typically support Secret vs Top Secret, and what is one depth difference?

Cue

Tier 3 → Secret/Confidential (~5 years, NAC/credit). Tier 5 → Top Secret (full field, ~10 years, subject interviews).

Q6

What is SEAD 4, and how many adjudicative guidelines does it define? Is it a checklist?

Cue

Security Executive Agent Directive 4 — thirteen guidelines under the whole-person concept; cumulative/contextual, not a yes/no checklist.

Q7

What May 2025 SF 328 change matters for labs with foreign research revenue? Approximate NISS deploy date?

Cue

Q7b foreign revenue/proceeds disclosure threshold lowered to 15% (from 30%). Form ~1 May 2025; NISS ~12 May 2025 [S3][S4].

Q8

True or false: Achieving CMMC Level 2 or moving to Microsoft 365 GCC High confers an FCL (or Secret ATO).

Cue

False. CMMC/GCC High are the uncleared/CUI wall (or cloud support). FCL/NISP and DAAPM/RMF are separate. Sibling: CMMC lab-floor pack.

Q9

Name the four DCSA learning domains used in this pack.

Cue

Industrial security/NISP · personnel vetting · information systems authorization (DAAPM/RMF) · counterintelligence.

Q10

Map one CDSE course ID to FCL and one to PCL. What portal hosts them?

Cue

IS140 Facility Clearances in the NISP · IS142 Personnel Clearances in the NISP · STEPP via CDSE [S5][S6][S8].

Q11

Possessing vs non-possessing FCL — one-line difference. Does non-possessing remove the need for KMPs?

Cue

Possessing may store/process classified on-site; non-possessing does not store on-site but still needs sponsorship, cleared KMPs, FSO, and an insider threat program.

Q12

What manual governs RMF-aligned authorization of classified contractor information systems under DCSA?

Cue

DAAPM (DCSA Assessment and Authorization Process Manual); CDSE CS250 covers applying A&A in the NISP [S13][S14].

Q13

In one sentence, state today’s falsifier (what would make the day a waste).

Cue

Ending the day still believing a company can self-sponsor an FCL, or that GCC High / CMMC / FedRAMP equals FCL (or authorizes Secret/TS).

Q14

What is DD Form 254, and who issues the need it documents?

Cue

Contract Security Classification Specification — GCA or cleared prime defines classified need-to-know, level, and scope.

Q15

What failure does uploading draft/placeholder forms before Day 20 signal?

Cue

Clock Theater — empty speed over completeness; package return and process reset [S2].

Q16

Continuous vetting vs legacy periodic reinvestigation for NISP contractors?

Cue

CV: ongoing automated checks + PVQ about every five years. Legacy PR calendar cycles eliminated for many NISP contractors [S9].

Q17

Finance says “we’re under 30% foreign proceeds” after May 2025 — what does that signal?

Cue

Team still on the old threshold; remodel to the 15% Q7b line [S4].

Q18

Name two FOCI mitigation instruments and one planning rule.

Cue

SCA, SSA, Voting Trust, or Proxy Agreement. Plan months of schedule risk; exact calendar windows are silence in primary sources.

Q19

Can an individual independently apply for and fund their own PCL without a facility sponsor?

Cue

No. PCL requires validated need-to-know initiated by an eligible facility sponsor.

Q20

Distinguish in one sentence each: CMMC floor, FCL, classified system ATO.

Cue

CMMC = CUI cybersecurity maturity; FCL = facility eligibility under NISP; ATO = system authorization under DAAPM/RMF.
