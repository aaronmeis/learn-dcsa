What DCSA is

The Defense Counterintelligence and Security Agency protects "America's trusted workforce, trusted workspaces, and classified information." Its two core missions are personnel vetting and industrial security, supported by counterintelligence, insider threat and security training [S36].

Where it came from

  • 1971: Defense Investigative Service (DIS) [S37]
  • 1980: industrial security moves to DIS [S37]
  • 1993: EO 12829 creates the NISP [S39]
  • 1999: DIS becomes the Defense Security Service (DSS) [S37]
  • 2005: investigations move to OPM [S37]
  • 2016: NBIB set up under OPM [S37]
  • 2019: EO 13869 directs the rename of DSS to DCSA [S38]; NBIB and the DoD CAF join effective Oct 1 2019 [S37][S40]

Why we do this

EO 12829: when contracts, licenses or grants need classified information, it must be "safeguarded in a manner equivalent to its protection within the executive branch of Government" [S39].
