DCSA (Defense Counterintelligence and Security Agency) — Tech Deep Dive Hub

Slug: dcsa · NBLM alias: tdd-dcsa · Parent: Tech Deep Dive Index

Business Explanation

One long day (~7h) to understand the DCSA dual-track operating model as an architect: facility clearance (FCL) and personnel clearance (PCL) interlocking under the NISP, with FOCI, SEAD 4 adjudication, DAAPM/RMF system authorization, and counterintelligence as supporting domains. The day ends in a design review that can separate a CMMC floor from an FCL and from a classified ATO — without running video or W8 in this fill pass.

TL;DR

  • Dual track: FCL and PCL feed each other; KMP roster is the hinge; neither track alone is enough. [S1][S2]
  • No self-sponsor: only a GCA or cleared prime opens NISS sponsorship. [S2][S10]
  • Clocks: Day 20 = complete facility package; Day 45 = KMP investigation initiation; FOCI mitigation adds months. [S2][S12]
  • FOCI 2025: SF 328 Q7(b) foreign-proceeds threshold 15% (was 30%); NISS live 12 May 2025. [S3][S4]
  • Four domains: NISP · personnel vetting · IS authorization (DAAPM/RMF) · CI. [S13][S14]
  • Do not confuse: CMMC (CUI floor) ≠ FCL (facility eligibility) ≠ ATO (system authorization).
  • AI: assistive checklists only; hard-block SF-86/FOCI PII; no AI sponsorship or adjudication.
  • Primaries win: academic FOCI literature is thin — see elicit-light-research.

Sibling: One-day mastery (pedagogy day) · claims shared shape in CLAIMS-AND-DEBATES.
