Logical View — DCSA (Defense Counterintelligence and Security Agency)

Logical view of DCSA dual-track: FCL/PCL process flows, data entities, Day 20 and DCSA due-date clocks — still vendor-neutral on hosting.

Altitude rule

Logical means which parts and how they talk, independent of where they run. Components have responsibilities and interfaces. Data entities have attributes and relationships. Prefer process and record names over portal brand names; named systems of record appear in the physical view.

Design decisions at this level

  • Treat sponsorship as a hard gate: no facility record foreign-interest amounts without GCA or cleared-prime need. [S2][S10]
  • Model FOCI as a branch, not a checkbox: determination can clear, mitigate, or deny. [S3][S4]
  • Keep KMP roster as a first-class entity — wrong officers omitted is a top failure mode. [S10]
  • Separate Tier 3 vs Tier 5 investigation scope in the PCL case attributes. [S6]
  • Place DAAG overlay on the IS authorization component so CMMC/FedRAMP language cannot substitute for classified ATO. [S13][S14]
  • Affirm the May 2025 conceptual change: foreign-foreign-interest amounts threshold for Q7(b) moves to 15% (physical form change lands in view 3). [S3][S4]
