Conceptual View — DCSA (Defense Counterintelligence and Security Agency)

Conceptual view of DCSA: four domains, dual FCL/PCL tracks, capabilities and actors only — no product names.

Pack: 00 - DCSA (Defense Counterintelligence and Security Agency) Hub · Block: Block 2 · Next: Logical view

Altitude rule

Conceptual means what and why, not how. Name capabilities and actors. Do not name products, portals, SKUs, or hostnames here. If a sentence needs a system name, it belongs in the physical view.

Adaptation

DCSA is a government operating model and program, not a single commercial platform. The four view notes adapt: conceptual = mission capabilities across four domains; physical = named systems of record and forms. Sources are ledger tags [S#] in the source ledger.

Problem and purpose

  • Cleared industry must protect classified information while remaining competitive. Without a single administrator of industrial security rules, each agency would invent its own facility and personnel eligibility process. [S1]
  • The binding constraint is dual eligibility: a facility that is eligible to hold or access classified information, and people who are eligible to access it — neither alone is enough. [S2][S5][S6]
  • Foreign ownership, control, or influence (FOCI) can make a otherwise-capable US organization ineligible until mitigated. [S3][S4][S12]
  • Who loses what if it is absent: the Government Contracting Activity loses a trusted industrial base; the uncleared company loses access to classified work; the cleared individual loses livelihood and trust; the public loses assurance that classified material stays controlled.

Key concepts (conceptual data)

Concept Meaning Related to
