Mission — DCSA (Defense Counterintelligence and Security Agency)

Why

One long, architecture-heavy day on the DCSA dual-track operating model: facility clearance (FCL) and personnel clearance (PCL) interlocking under the National Industrial Security Program (NISP), with FOCI, adjudication, IS authorization, and counterintelligence as the supporting domains. [S1][S2]

  • Can place DAAG/RMF on the IS-authorization branch and say how it overlays NIST RMF for contractor classified systems [S13][S14]
  • Can distinguish the CMMC floor (CUI cybersecurity) from FCL eligibility and from a classified system ATO
  • Can list the third-party systems of record and how sponsorship, investigation, training, and A&A connect
