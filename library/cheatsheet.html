Cheatsheet — DCSA dual-track (FCL / PCL)

One-page cheatsheet — clocks, package, SEAD 4, SF 328 15%, CDSE map.

Dual-track lock

Track Start System End state
FCL GCA or cleared prime sponsors in NISS (no self-sponsor) NISS + package Facility cleared at level
PCL Need-to-know + sponsor initiates investigation SF-86 via NBIS Person eligible; then briefed/accessed

No useful PCL pipeline without an eligible facility sponsor; no usable FCL without cleared KMPs / need-to-know population.

Day 20 / Day 45 clocks

Welcome email = Day 1.

Clock Due What
Day 20 Governance + forms package in NISS Formation/legal docs · ownership chart · DD-441 · SF-328 · KMP list
Day 45 KMP investigation requests + fingerprints Uncleared KMPs queued for PCL processing

Rule: Incomplete packages get returned — quality beats “fast empty.”

Package checklist

  • Formation / legal docs
  • Ownership / org chart (beneficial owners)
  • DD Form 441 — Security Agreement
  • SF 328 — Foreign interests (FOCI)
  • KMP list (roles clear)
  • Fingerprint / investigation-request readiness for KMPs

SF 328 — May 2025

  • Form approved ~1 May 2025; NISS live ~12 May 2025 [S3]
  • Foreign revenue / proceeds disclosure threshold (Q7b) lowered to 15% (from 30%) [S4]
  • Material changes → update; “no FOCI” is not permanent

SEAD 4 — thirteen guidelines (whole person)

  1. Allegiance to the United States
  2. Foreign influence
  3. Foreign preference
  4. Sexual behavior
  5. Personal conduct
  6. Financial considerations
  7. Alcohol consumption
  8. Drug involvement and substance misuse
  9. Psychological conditions
  10. Criminal conduct
  11. Handling protected information
  12. Outside activities
  13. Use of information technology systems

Decision rules

  1. When someone says “we’ll sponsor ourselves,” answer: GCA or cleared prime in NISS.
  2. When cloud/CMMC comes up, answer: CMMC / GCC High / FedRAMP ≠ FCL (and ≠ Secret/TS ATO).
  3. When foreign revenue ~15%+, answer: revisit SF 328 Q7 before Day 20 and on material change.
  4. When “we’re non-possessing so light touch,” answer: still need sponsorship, KMPs, FSO program.
  5. When classified systems are in scope, answer: DAAPM/RMF, not CMMC alone.

Top traps

Trap Fix
Self-sponsor myth Get GCA/prime sponsorship
GCC High / CMMC = cleared Separate CUI floor from NISP FCL/ATO
SF 328 one-and-done Update on material change; note 15%
Day 20 empty package Complete five-pack before submit
Skip KMP fingerprints past Day 45 Queue investigations early
