Block 1: Context and scope

Block 1 — DCSA context and scope: dual-track boundary, neighbors, CMMC vs FCL vs ATO.

Time: 45 minutes · Goal: What the operating model is, where its boundary sits, and why the dual tracks exist

Focus from mind map

  • What it is: DoD agency administering the NISP for most cleared industry, plus personnel vetting / continuous vetting and related CI mission elements.
  • Central thesis: Two interlocking tracks — FCL and PCL — plus IS authorization (DAAPM/RMF) and counterintelligence. Neither track is sufficient alone.
  • Out of scope for this day: Full CMMC memorization; SAP/SCI deep dive; building a classified enclave from scratch.
  • Cannot self-sponsor — sponsorship via NISS by GCA or cleared prime. [S2][S10]
  • SF 328 2025: Q7(b) foreign proceeds threshold 15% (was 30%); NISS deploy 12 May 2025. [S3][S4]

Sources

  • [S1] eCFR 32 CFR Part 117 NISPOM
  • [S2] DCSA FCL Orientation Handbook (2026)
  • [S3][S4] SF 328 May 2025 announcement + industry paper
  • [S5][S6] CDSE IS140 / IS142
  • Full ledger: source-ledger

Actions (tied to views)

  • Write a two-sentence definition of the DCSA dual-track model and a one-line boundary (in: NISP FCL/PCL/FOCI/DAAPM/CI; out: CMMC cram, SAP/SCI build).
  • List ≥5 neighbor systems/regimes on the boundary: NISS, NBIS/e-QIP, personnel SoR, STEPP, eMASS, plus CMMC/FedRAMP as adjacent, not inside.
  • Sketch the four domains (NISP, vetting, IS auth, CI) on one page before opening 01 - Conceptual View.
  • Tag each ledger row to the view it feeds (conceptual / logical / physical / integration).
  • Write the decision rule: CMMC floor ≠ FCL ≠ classified ATO — one sentence each.

Retrieval check

Question

Where exactly is the boundary of this DCSA deep dive, and what sits just outside it?

Answer cues

In: NISP facility eligibility, personnel vetting/adjudication/CV, FOCI (SF 328), DAAPM/RMF for contractor classified systems, CI reporting culture. Sponsorship only by GCA or cleared prime — no self-sponsor. Outside: CMMC control memorization, FedRAMP/GCC High as if they were FCLs, SAP/SCI enclave engineering, commercial GRC product tours. Neighbors: NISS, NBIS/e-QIP, DISS-class personnel SoR [verify], STEPP, eMASS, DD 254 contracting, cloud hosts.

Exit criteria

  • Boundary statement written; neighbor list has at least five systems; CMMC/FCL/ATO distinction written in affirmative language.
